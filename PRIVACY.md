# Privacy

The UX Design Workbench plugin contains instructions and a local validation script. Version 1.0 has no MCP server, user account, analytics service, or plugin-operated data store.

The plugin does not automatically send product evidence or Theme Manifests to the public Design System Atlas. Users choose what they provide to their AI host, and that host's privacy terms apply.

The public Atlas runs in the browser. It may request Google Fonts when users preview typography and may link to third-party design-system references. Those third parties receive ordinary web-request information under their own policies.

Do not provide secrets, credentials, regulated personal data, or confidential production data unless your chosen AI host and working environment are approved for that information.
